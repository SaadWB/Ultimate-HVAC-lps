Your AC System Is Only as Good as Its Installation

Even the highest-rated air conditioner will fail prematurely if improperly sized or poorly installed. Here is why proper installation matters for your home.

Proper System Sizing

Oversized systems short-cycle and leave uncomfortable humidity, while undersized units run constantly. We calculate exact BTUs to ensure balanced comfort.

Precision Calibration

Accurate refrigerant line charging, pressure testing, and factory-spec electrical wiring prevent early compressor failure and maximize seasonal SEER2 ratings.

Airflow Optimization

Sealing duct leaks and setting static air pressure properly eliminates hot spots in upstairs bedrooms and ensures whisper-quiet airflow throughout your home.

Long-Term Reliability

Proper professional installation maintains full manufacturer warranty compliance, lowers monthly power bills, and delivers 15+ years of dependable cooling.

AC Installation FAQs

Common questions homeowners in Skokie and Cook County have about our AC installation services, financing, and warranties.

If your cooling system is over 10–12 years old, requires frequent repairs, uses phased-out R-22 refrigerant, or struggles to maintain comfortable temperatures across all rooms, a replacement is often the most cost-effective decision. Our technicians diagnose your system honestly first and help you weigh repair vs. replacement without high-pressure sales.
Most residential central AC installations or direct system replacements take between 4 to 8 hours (typically completed within a single day). Complex multi-zone ductless mini-split setups or extensive duct modifications may take slightly longer. We always give you a clear schedule in advance.
We perform an accurate cooling load evaluation that factors in your home’s square footage, window exposure, insulation levels, ceiling height, and ductwork capacity. Proper sizing prevents short-cycling, controls indoor humidity, and ensures optimal energy efficiency.
Yes! Ultimate HVAC offers flexible financing options (subject to credit approval) to fit your household budget. Options include deferred interest / same-as-cash promotional periods, affordable low monthly payments, and long-term financing for complete HVAC system replacements.
